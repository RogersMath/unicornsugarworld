What js13kGames is

js13kGames is an annual competition, run since 2012 by Andrzej Mazur, where every entry has to fit in 13,312 bytes once it is zipped. The whole game: code, art, sound, everything. There are no downloads and no libraries pulled from the web. The theme is announced on 13 August and the deadline is 13 September. This year's theme was Unicorns and Rainbows.

Thirteen kilobytes is about six pages of plain text. You cannot store pictures or sounds at that size, so every image and every note has to be made by a formula when the page loads. That constraint is the point. It strips away the parts of game-making that stop people from finishing, and leaves the parts that matter.

I am not a coder, a writer, or an artist. I directed these the way a producer directs a film: deciding what each game is, what it should feel like, and whether the result is any good, while an AI partner wrote the code. If that way of working interests you, the post-mortem explains how it went, in plain language, including what did not work.

The story, in order

  1. Rainbow Tiles: a cozy apprenticeship in a world of dancing unicorns.
  2. Sugarwind: Athena, who has only one wing, learns to sail the sugar dunes with it, and sails too far.
  3. Looking Up: she wakes in the web of Anansi, who will tell her the truth if she can get it out of him.
  4. Unicorn Apocalypse: the truth, seen from the wrong side. You are the thing under the tombs.
  5. Deathball: she goes down to seal it, with seven flutes and a cage of sticks.

The first two are gentle. The last three get dark. Every game stands alone, but they land harder in order.

Play

Each game links to its js13kGames entry page, where you can play it and leave the developer feedback that decides the results. Playable copies will move here after judging.

Cozy · puzzle

Rainbow Tiles

Push raw sugar into colour under Master Praline's eye. A tile puzzle that grows into a small deck of tricks.

Phone and desktop · touch or mouse

Surf · arcade

Sugarwind

One wing, one board, one minute. Catch the gusts, ride the dunes, and do not look at what is following you.

Phone and desktop · swipe or A/D

Conversation · negotiation

Looking Up

Anansi never lies, and never tells the whole story. Move his mood, put two of his words together, and ask. You have until dark.

Phone and desktop · tap · text heavy

Rail · VR

Unicorn Apocalypse

You are what killed the unicorns. Drain their colour, let go when they turn grey, and grow the flock that flies beside you.

Desktop, phone, or a WebXR headset · contains bright colour and fast motion; reduced-flashing mode in settings

Physics · action

Deathball

Roll a cage of sticks and web through a tomb. Bump seven flutes onto their notches. Crush what gets in the way.

Phone and desktop · swipe or mouse
